THE FINE PRINT, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Local finds.
Clear sources.

Every refresh asks our server to read the official pages behind this shortlist. We check posted pricing and structured event data, then show a check status for each listing. This is a curated directory, not a search of every Denver business.

Posted doesn’t mean guaranteed.

A price found on a page is labeled “Source checked.” It does not confirm a free appointment, remaining tickets, or an unexpired promotion without a published end date. Contact the business before going. Regular menu prices and shop minimums are labeled separately from offers.

What can update?

Supported price fields, published social links, structured business hours, and event dates refresh from the source. New Denver BAZAAR dates can appear from its official calendar. Other shops are part of the curated list. Unreadable or changed pages are flagged; expired or cancelled events are hidden.

Distance & hours

Distances are approximate straight-line miles from Denver Union Station, using approximate venue coordinates. They are not travel distances. Hours are labeled as researched unless freshly read from structured data; holiday hours may differ.

On your phone

Install from your browser menu, or use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Saved places stay in this browser. The last loaded listings remain available offline; fresh prices require a connection. Editorial photos illustrate categories and do not represent individual shops.

Initial research: October 28, 2026. No accounts, location access, or paid API keys required.
